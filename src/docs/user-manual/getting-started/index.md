# Getting started

Start here if you are new to the Orchard Core admin, whatever your job on the site. These pages show how the admin screens are laid out, how to sign in and look after your own account, and why two people can see different menus. Then pick the training path for your role.

The admin is at `/Admin` on your site. Your own account (profile, password, two-factor authentication) is in the user menu, the person icon at the top right of every admin page.
